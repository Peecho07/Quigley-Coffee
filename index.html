Our story

Coffee is our thing; people are our heart.

Michael Quigley and his wife both grew up in Sanford. They opened the shop on a side of town that did not have specialty coffee yet.

Inside it is bright and colorful, with living-room seating and a community table that seats 14. Check the corkboard of guest Polaroids. Regulars use it to spot friends who came in before them.
