Menu questions

Before you order

Where can I see today's prices?

Prices live in the order app so they are always current. Tap Order ahead, browse, and close it if you would rather order at the counter.

Do you have dairy-free milk?

Yes. Alt milks are available for any drink for an extra charge.

Can I order before I leave home?

Yes. Place a mobile order and it will be ready at the counter when you arrive.

Is the Koda Brew available hot?

No. The Koda Brew is served cold only.
