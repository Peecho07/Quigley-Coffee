The fine print

Q Club questions

How often can I use it?

Once every two hours. Each order covers one drink, one pastry and one sandwich.

Can I share my membership?

No. Q Club is tied to your profile and the benefits are not transferable.

Does it work on mobile orders?

Not yet. Benefits are redeemed in person at the shop for now.

How am I billed?

You are charged $12 every week and the membership renews automatically.

How do I cancel or update my card?

Open your subscription preferences at club.quigleycoffeeco.com to change payment details or cancel.
