Planning questions

What hosts ask before they book

What does the venue need to provide?

One standard 120v outlet. The barista brings the cart, the supplies and everything else.

How many guests can one barista serve?

One barista makes 50 to 60 drinks per hour. Add a second barista for another 30 drinks per hour.

Is gratuity included in the $795?

No. The Signature Coffee Cart starts at $795 plus a 10% gratuity.

We expect more than 300 guests. Can you handle it?

Yes. Events with 300+ guests get a custom quote within 24 to 48 hours.

How far ahead should I order printed cups?

Full-wrap printed cups need a 6-week lead time.
